# Ideation Frameworks and Templates

Detailed worksheets referenced by the `ideation` skill. Use these when filling in Steps 4-7 of `SKILL.md`.

## Audience Segment Dimensions (Step 4)

For each of the 3-5 primary segments, document:

- **Demographics**: age range, gender distribution, income level, education, location (urban/suburban/rural), family status
- **Psychographics**: values, attitudes, lifestyle choices, personality traits, motivations
- **Behaviors**: tech usage patterns, app habits, purchasing behavior, media consumption, social media presence
- **Pain intensity**: how badly they need this solution (1-10 scale with justification)
- **Willingness to pay**: estimated price sensitivity and spending habits on similar products
- **Acquisition channel**: where you can reach them (social media, search, communities, influencers, partnerships)

Then: rank segments by priority and name the beachhead market (which to target first, and why); estimate segment sizes with reasoning; identify secondary/future segments to expand into later.

## User Persona Template (Step 5)

Each of the 3-5 personas needs all seven parts:

1. **Identity**: full name (fictional but realistic), age, occupation and industry, location (city, country), family status, income bracket
2. **Profile**: brief bio (2-3 sentences), daily routine summary, tech comfort level (1-10 with description, e.g. "7 - uses apps daily but avoids complex tools"), devices used
3. **Goals** (3-5): what they want to achieve, both immediate/tactical and deeper/emotional
4. **Pain points** (3-5): specific frustrations in the problem space, current workarounds and why those are inadequate
5. **Behavioral patterns**: how they currently try to solve the problem, what triggers them to seek a solution, what would make them switch, what would make them recommend the product
6. **Product usage scenario**: a concrete story of discovery, onboarding, and first-week use; which feature they use first and why; what turns them into a paying customer
7. **Subscription likelihood**: which pricing tier they would choose and why, what would make them upgrade/downgrade, churn risk factors

## Success Metrics Checklist (Step 6)

- **Acquisition**: monthly new signups (Month 1/3/6/12), cost per acquisition (CPA), signup-to-activation conversion, channel-specific targets (organic, paid, referral)
- **Engagement**: DAU/MAU ratio, average session duration, feature adoption rate per feature, retention (Day 1, Day 7, Day 30)
- **Revenue**: Monthly Recurring Revenue targets (Month 3/6/12), Average Revenue Per User, free-to-paid conversion rate, monthly churn rate
- **Product-specific**: 3-5 metrics unique to the core value proposition, each with a target and a reason it validates product-market fit
- **North Star metric**: one metric that best captures the core value delivered, why it beats the alternatives, and a 12-month target
