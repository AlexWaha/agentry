# Deck Template - Slide-by-Slide Content

The standard investor deck has 10-12 slides. Each slide must be concise -
investors see hundreds of decks. Every slide earns the right to the next.

## Slide Structure

| Slide # | Title | Purpose | Time (30s-1min each) |
|---------|-------|---------|----------------------|
| 1 | Title | First impression, company name, tagline | 15s |
| 2 | Problem | Why this matters | 45s |
| 3 | Solution | What we built | 45s |
| 4 | Demo / Product | Show, don't tell | 60s |
| 5 | Market Size | How big is the opportunity | 45s |
| 6 | Business Model | How we make money | 45s |
| 7 | Traction | Proof it works | 45s |
| 8 | Competition | Why we win | 45s |
| 9 | Team | Why we can execute | 30s |
| 10 | Financial Projections | Where we're headed | 45s |
| 11 | The Ask | What we need | 30s |
| 12 | Thank You / Contact | Close | 15s |

Total presentation: ~8-10 minutes (standard pitch length).

For EACH slide, produce:
- **Title**: Bold, short (3-6 words)
- **Subtitle**: One explanatory line (optional)
- **Bullet Points**: 3-5 maximum per slide. Each bullet is one line, impactful.
- **Key Visual Description**: What chart, image, or diagram should be on this slide
- **Speaker Notes**: 3-5 sentences of what the presenter says while this slide is showing. Conversational tone, includes data points and transitions to next slide.

---

**Slide 1: Title Slide**

Content:
- Product/company name (large)
- Tagline / one-liner (from product vision)
- Presenter name and title
- Date
- Optional: a single striking visual or product screenshot

Speaker Notes:
- Opening hook (surprising stat or relatable story)
- Quick context: who we are, what we do
- Transition: "Let me tell you about the problem we're solving..."

---

**Slide 2: The Problem**

Content:
- Headline: "X million people struggle with [problem]" (or similar attention-grabbing stat)
- 3-4 bullet points describing the pain:
  - Pain point 1 with statistic
  - Pain point 2 with statistic
  - Why current solutions fail (one bullet)
  - Cost of the problem (time, money, or health impact)
- Visual: infographic or icon set illustrating the pain

Speaker Notes:
- Tell a brief story of a real person (use persona) experiencing this problem
- Reference the statistics on slide
- Build empathy: "You probably know someone like this"
- Transition: "We built something to fix this."

---

**Slide 3: The Solution**

Content:
- Headline: product name + one sentence of what it does
- 3-4 key features (icon + short description for each)
- USP highlighted prominently
- Visual: product screenshot or conceptual illustration

Speaker Notes:
- Explain the product in simple terms
- Highlight the unique innovation (the thing competitors don't have)
- Connect each feature back to a problem from slide 2
- Transition: "Let me show you how it works..."

---

**Slide 4: Demo / Product**

Content:
- 2-3 product screenshots or mockup images (described in detail for PPTX generation)
- Brief captions under each screenshot
- User journey: 3 steps (Sign up -> Create -> Track) or similar

Speaker Notes:
- Walk through the user experience step by step
- Highlight the "magic moment" - when users first feel value
- Mention the print-digital bridge if applicable
- Transition: "Now let's talk about the size of this opportunity..."

---

**Slide 5: Market Size**

Content:
- TAM / SAM / SOM visual (concentric circles or funnel)
- Each with dollar amount and brief label
- Market growth rate (CAGR) prominently displayed
- Key growth driver (one line)

Speaker Notes:
- Explain each market tier briefly
- Emphasize the growth trajectory
- Reference credible sources for the numbers
- Transition: "Here's how we capture our share..."

---

**Slide 6: Business Model**

Content:
- Revenue model type: "SaaS Subscription"
- Pricing tiers (visual comparison):
  - Free tier: what's included
  - Tier 1: price + what's included
  - Tier 2: price + what's included
- Key unit economics: ARPU, gross margin
- Secondary revenue streams (one line each)

Speaker Notes:
- Explain pricing rationale (benchmarked against competitors)
- Highlight gross margin
- Mention expansion revenue potential (upsell, print partnerships)
- Transition: "And we're already seeing early signals..."

---

**Slide 7: Traction / Validation**

Content:
- If pre-launch: validation signals (waitlist size, beta feedback, market research findings, landing page conversion rate)
- If post-launch: key metrics (users, MRR, growth rate, retention)
- Timeline graphic showing progress milestones
- Social proof: notable beta users, press mentions, community engagement

Speaker Notes:
- Present the strongest proof of demand
- If pre-launch, be honest but confident: "We've validated demand through..."
- Show momentum - things are moving in the right direction
- Transition: "Now let's look at who we're up against..."

---

**Slide 8: Competition**

Content:
- 2x2 positioning matrix (two most important axes)
- Our product positioned in the ideal quadrant (top-right typically)
- Competitors plotted on the matrix
- 2-3 bullet points on key differentiators
- Do NOT badmouth competitors - show why we're different, not why they're bad

Speaker Notes:
- Acknowledge competitors exist (investors know they do)
- Explain positioning axes and why they matter
- Highlight the unique space we occupy
- Mention defensibility (what moats exist)
- Transition: "Here's the team making this happen..."

---

**Slide 9: Team**

Content:
- Founder/team photos (placeholder descriptions for PPTX)
- Name, title, key credential for each person (one line)
- Relevant experience highlights (previous companies, domain expertise, years of experience)
- Advisory board (if applicable)

Speaker Notes:
- Brief intro of each team member and their superpower
- Why this specific team is uniquely positioned to solve this problem
- Mention relevant domain expertise
- Transition: "Let me share where we're headed financially..."

---

**Slide 10: Financial Projections**

Content:
- Revenue projection chart (Year 1-3 or Year 1-5)
- Key metrics table: Year 1 / Year 2 / Year 3
  - Users (total)
  - Paying users
  - MRR / ARR
  - Gross margin
- Breakeven point highlighted

Speaker Notes:
- Walk through revenue trajectory
- Highlight conservative assumptions (build credibility)
- Point to breakeven timeline
- Mention unit economics: LTV:CAC ratio
- Transition: "To get there, here's what we need..."

---

**Slide 11: The Ask**

Content:
- Funding amount (large, prominent)
- Use of funds breakdown (pie chart or bar chart):
  - Product development: X%
  - Marketing/growth: X%
  - Operations: X%
  - Buffer: X%
- Key milestones this funding enables (3-4 bullets)
- Expected runway (months)

Speaker Notes:
- State the ask clearly and confidently
- Explain what each dollar category achieves
- Connect milestones to next fundraising stage or profitability
- Create urgency: why invest now, not later
- Transition: "Thank you for your time. I'd love to answer your questions."

---

**Slide 12: Thank You / Contact**

Content:
- "Thank you" or product name + tagline
- Contact information (email, phone, website)
- QR code linking to product landing page or demo
- Social media handles

Speaker Notes:
- Brief closing statement
- Open the floor for questions
- Mention next steps: "I'll send the deck and our financial model after this meeting"

## Python PPTX Generation Script

Create `docs/presentations/scripts/generate_pitch.py` - a complete, runnable
Python script. It must:

1. **Be self-contained**: Single file, clear comments, easy to modify.
2. **Install instructions**: Comments at the top explaining `pip install python-pptx`.
3. **Branding configuration**: Constants at the top for easy customization:
   - Primary color (hex)
   - Secondary color (hex)
   - Accent color (hex)
   - Font family (title and body)
   - Company name
   - Tagline
4. **Slide dimensions**: Widescreen 16:9 (standard investor deck).
5. **Consistent layout**: Each slide uses a consistent template:
   - Title area (top or left)
   - Content area (center/right)
   - Slide number (bottom right)
   - Company logo placeholder (top right or bottom left)
6. **Slide generation functions**: One function per slide type:
   - `create_title_slide()` - centered title, subtitle, contact info
   - `create_content_slide(title, bullets, notes)` - standard bullet slide
   - `create_two_column_slide(title, left_content, right_content)` - for comparison slides
   - `create_chart_placeholder_slide(title, chart_description)` - for financial/market slides with placeholder shapes
   - `create_team_slide(members)` - grid layout for team photos/bios
   - `create_thank_you_slide()` - closing slide with contact
7. **Content embedded in script**: All slide content embedded directly in the
   script (not reading from .md files - makes it portable).
8. **Speaker notes**: Added to each slide via python-pptx notes API.
9. **Output path**: `docs/presentations/output/pitch-deck.pptx`
10. **Error handling**: Graceful error messages if python-pptx is not installed.
