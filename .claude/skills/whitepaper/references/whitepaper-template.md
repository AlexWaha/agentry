# Whitepaper Section Templates

Full outline and sub-bullets for every section of the whitepaper. Use this
while drafting each step in SKILL.md; do not copy verbatim, synthesize.

## Vision and Mission Statement

**Vision Statement** (1-2 sentences):
- Where the world is headed and how this product fits into that future
- Aspirational but grounded - 5-10 year horizon
- Should inspire but also be believable

**Mission Statement** (1-2 sentences):
- What the company does today to move toward the vision
- Specific, actionable, measurable
- Focused on the customer benefit

**Core Values** (3-5 values):
- Each value in 2-3 words with a one-sentence explanation
- These should inform product decisions, hiring, and partnerships
- Must be genuine differentiators, not generic platitudes

## Market Context and Opportunity

Synthesize market research into a compelling narrative (NOT a copy-paste of
market-research.md):

1. **The Macro Trend**: What large-scale shift creates the opportunity? (e.g.,
   wellness movement, digital-physical convergence, habit science awareness)
2. **Market Size**: TAM/SAM/SOM in one paragraph with key numbers. Reference
   the full market research for details.
3. **Growth Drivers**: 3-4 forces accelerating market growth.
4. **The Gap**: What the market is missing - the specific gap that creates the
   opportunity for this product. Connect competitor weaknesses to user
   frustrations.
5. **Timing**: Why now is the right moment. What has changed in the last 2-3
   years that makes this viable now but not 5 years ago?
6. **Evidence of Demand**: Any data points showing users actively seeking this
   type of solution (search trends, community discussions, competitor growth
   rates, survey data).

## The Problem (Detailed)

Write a deep, empathetic problem analysis. This section should make the
reader nod and think "yes, I've experienced this" or "yes, I know someone
like this."

1. **The Core Problem** (1 paragraph): Precise statement of the pain.
2. **The Human Story**: Describe a day in the life of someone struggling with
   this problem. Use persona details but make it feel like a real story, not
   a persona card.
3. **Statistics and Evidence**:
   - How many people face this problem (global and target market)
   - Success rates with current methods (low - cite sources)
   - Economic cost (time, money, health impact)
   - Emotional cost (frustration, guilt, abandonment)
4. **Why Current Solutions Fail**:
   - Digital-only solutions: explain limitations (screen fatigue,
     out-of-sight-out-of-mind, notification blindness)
   - Paper-only solutions: explain limitations (no tracking, no reminders, no
     accountability)
   - Generic tools: explain limitations (not designed for habit tracking, too
     complex, no motivation loop)
5. **The Missing Piece**: What insight about human behavior or technology
   makes the current solution landscape incomplete? This sets up the product
   as the answer.

## Our Solution (Product Overview)

Present the product as the answer to the problem defined above:

1. **Product Overview** (1-2 paragraphs): What it is, what it does, and who
   it's for. Written for a non-technical audience.
2. **Key Features** (5-8 features): For each feature:
   - Feature name
   - One-sentence description
   - Which problem it solves (reference the Problem section)
   - Why it matters to the user
   - What makes our implementation unique
3. **The Unique Innovation**: The one thing that sets this product apart from
   everything else. Explain it in depth - why it works, what insight it's
   based on, why competitors haven't done it.
4. **User Journey**: Walk through a complete user journey from discovery to
   daily usage:
   - Discovery (how they find the product)
   - Onboarding (first 5 minutes)
   - First value moment (when they feel "this is worth it")
   - Daily engagement loop
   - Habit formation (when the product becomes essential)
   - Advocacy (when they tell others about it)
5. **Platform Strategy**: Brief overview of web + mobile + print approach and
   why multi-platform matters.

## Technology Overview (High-Level)

Present the technology approach at a strategic level. NO code, NO
implementation details. This is for investors and partners, not developers.

1. **Architecture Philosophy**: What principles guide the technical design
   (reliability, scalability, security, performance). 2-3 sentences each.
2. **Technology Stack**: Brief overview of key technology choices and WHY
   each was chosen (not just WHAT):
   - Backend framework and language (why this over alternatives)
   - Database choice (why this for the data model)
   - Mobile approach (why this for cross-platform)
   - Infrastructure approach (scalability, cost-efficiency)
3. **Scalability Plan**: How the system grows from 100 users to 1M users.
   Three stages:
   - Stage 1 (0-10K users): architecture and costs
   - Stage 2 (10K-100K users): what changes
   - Stage 3 (100K-1M users): what changes
4. **Data Security and Privacy**: How user data is protected. Compliance
   considerations (GDPR, data residency).
5. **Innovation Edge**: Any technical innovations that create defensibility
   (e.g., OCR scanning accuracy, print-digital sync technology, recommendation
   engine).

## Competitive Advantages

Build a compelling case for why this product wins:

1. **Feature Comparison Matrix**: Simplified version of the competitive
   landscape matrix. Include only the top 5 competitors and the most
   differentiating features.
2. **Competitive Moats**: What advantages are hard for competitors to
   replicate:
   - Network effects (referral QR codes create viral loops)
   - Data advantage (print+digital usage data is unique)
   - Brand positioning (occupying a unique niche)
   - Switching costs (data, habits, printed materials)
   - Technology barriers (print-digital bridge)
3. **Competitive Response Analysis**: How will competitors likely respond?
   What is our counter-strategy?
4. **First-Mover Advantages**: If applicable, what benefits come from being
   first in this specific niche.

## Monetization Strategy

Synthesize the revenue model into a clear monetization narrative:

1. **Pricing Philosophy**: Why the pricing is set where it is. Value-based
   reasoning.
2. **Subscription Tiers**: Detailed description of each tier:
   - What's included
   - Target persona
   - Price and billing options
3. **Free Tier Strategy**: What's free, why it's free, and how it drives paid
   conversion.
4. **Revenue Diversification**: Beyond subscriptions - print partnerships,
   premium content, enterprise.
5. **Unit Economics Summary**: CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC ratio, gross margin.
   Reference financial model for details.
6. **Revenue Projections**: High-level Year 1-3 revenue trajectory (reference
   financial model for details).
7. **Path to Profitability**: When the business becomes profitable and what
   drives that inflection.

## Product Roadmap

Present a phased delivery plan:

**Phase 1: MVP (Months 1-4)**
- Core features delivered
- Target metrics (users, engagement)
- Key milestones

**Phase 2: Growth (Months 5-8)**
- Features that drive retention and virality
- Mobile app launch
- Target metrics

**Phase 3: Expansion (Months 9-12)**
- Print service integration
- Advanced features (AI recommendations, analytics)
- Target metrics

**Phase 4: Scale (Year 2)**
- Market expansion (geographies, languages)
- Enterprise/team features
- Partnership ecosystem
- Revenue targets

**Phase 5: Platform (Year 3+)**
- Long-term vision features
- Platform ecosystem
- Market leadership goals

For each phase, include: features, timeline, key milestones, success metrics,
and dependencies.

## Team and Execution

1. **Team Philosophy**: What kind of team is needed to execute this vision.
2. **Current Capabilities**: What skills and experience the founding team
   brings.
3. **Key Hires**: Roles needed and when, in priority order.
4. **Advisory Needs**: What expertise would benefit from advisors (industry,
   technology, growth).
5. **Execution Track Record**: Any relevant experience, achievements, or
   domain expertise that builds confidence in execution ability.

## Conclusion and Call to Action

1. **Summary**: Recap the opportunity in 2-3 sentences.
2. **Why This Product**: The strongest argument for why this product will
   succeed.
3. **Why This Team**: The strongest argument for why this team can execute.
4. **Why Now**: The strongest argument for why the timing is right.
5. **Call to Action**: What the reader should do next (invest, partner, join
   the beta, schedule a meeting).
